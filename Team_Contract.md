## Decision-Making Process

All team members have the power to initiate making a decision. Upon initiation of the decision-making process, the team will discuss the sides and possible outcomes of the situation, using a decision-making matrix or point system if necessary. If a resolution can't be reached by the team members alone, the advisors (Dr. Jillian Aurisano and Todd Lieberman) will be briefed on the decision that must be made, its possible outcomes, and the conflicting sides held by each member, and the advisors will provide their guidance to resolve the conflict and come to a decision.

## Conflict-Resolution Escalation Plan

Upon recognition of a conflict, team members will address it directly by discussing it amongst themselves using the crucial conversation framework (state your perspective on the situation as truthfully as possible, confirm you are working with the same information, identify shared motivations, decide on actions going forward). If the conflict does not get resolved through discussion between team members, advisors Dr. Jillian Aurisano and Todd Lieberman should get involved at the next possible opportunity. If the advisors are unable to resolve the conflict, the instructor should get involved as soon as possible.
